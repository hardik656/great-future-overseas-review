Great Future Overseas Chandigarh Review: ₹5,000 Taken, Fake New Zealand Job Offer (Oct 2026)

Summary of my experience: In October 2026 I paid Great Future Overseas (website greatfutureoverseas.in, office at SCO 3 Sector 41-D Chandigarh, phone +91 91159 49400) ₹5,000 as a “file login” fee for a New Zealand work visa. The job offer I then received was for a role at Allied Faxi New Zealand Food Co., and Allied Faxi told me in writing that the offer was not authorised by them. I was promised a refund, but it has been refused.

What happened, step by step

  1. October 2026: Great Future Overseas gave me a handwritten quote of ₹7 lakh in total for a New Zealand food packer job on a work visa, and asked for my passport, Aadhaar and certificates.
  2. 24 Oct 2026: I paid ₹5,000 by UPI in three payments (₹2,000, ₹2,000 and ₹1,000). They gave me receipt no. 1126, purpose “file login”, stamped “Great Future Overseas”.
  3. 28 Oct 2026: I received an email from infointernationimmigartionlawy@gmail.com, signed “Karan Bhardwaj, Immigration Lawyer, Behalf Of International Placement Company”, offering me a Shift Manager role at Allied Faxi New Zealand Food Co. for NZ$4,260 a month. It quoted my passport number, which I had given only to Great Future Overseas. It was CC’d to akshitasharma241994@gmail.com.
  4. 29 Oct 2026: I wrote to Allied Faxi directly. They (account@faxi.co.nz) replied in writing:
    “We have not engaged International Placement Company or Karan Bhardwaj to recruit on our behalf. The job offer you described is not authorised by our company. Please be alert to potential fraud and do not make any payments or provide personal documents to them.”
  5. 30 Oct 2026: I forwarded Allied Faxi’s reply to the sender of the offer and asked for an explanation.
  6. I was promised a refund of my ₹5,000, but it has been refused.

Because the employer itself has said in writing that it did not authorise this offer, I consider it a fraudulent job offer.

What I have done

  • Reported it to the Chandigarh Police cyber cell.
  • Reported it to the Ministry of External Affairs (Protector General of Emigrants and Protector of Emigrants, Chandigarh).
  • Posted a public complaint: ConsumerComplaints.in complaint about Great Future Overseas.

How to protect yourself

  • Check the agent’s licence on emigrate.gov.in before paying anything. Licensed recruiting agents are listed there.
  • Verify the employer directly using contact details from the company’s own website, not from the offer email.
  • Never pay a “file login” or registration fee before you have a verified offer from a real employer.
  • Be careful with offers sent from free Gmail addresses or signed by a “lawyer” on behalf of a placement company.
  • Report fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
